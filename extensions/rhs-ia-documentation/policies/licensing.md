# Licensing and Attribution Policy

The upstream Positron website states that its educational materials are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. The Positron name and icon are separately identified as trademarks of Posit Software, PBC.

Adapted documentation must preserve source provenance, identify that modifications were made for RHS-IA, retain the compatible share-alike license, and make attribution available offline.

Upstream trademarks and artwork are not RHS-IA branding assets. They must not be copied into the shipped extension except where an explicitly reviewed attribution use requires them.

The full offline attribution surface is a later compliance milestone. Phase A records the policy and upstream baseline needed to implement it correctly.
