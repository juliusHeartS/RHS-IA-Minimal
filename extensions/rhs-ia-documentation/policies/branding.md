# Branding Policy

RHS-IA product screenshots and product marks must use approved RHS-IA assets. Upstream screenshots containing Positron branding default to `RECREATE`.

Occurrences of Posit, Positron, `posit.co`, `positron:`, or `positron.` require review. They may remain only for accurate attribution, history, third-party product names, or verified internal identifiers.

Commands, settings, protocols, service URLs, and displayed UI labels must be checked against the application. They must not be mechanically renamed.

The Phase A prototype uses the approved RHS-IA light and dark logo files from the workspace `assets/` directory. Their source paths and hashes are recorded by the build process.
